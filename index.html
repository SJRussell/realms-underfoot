Realms Underfoot

A role-playing game you play outside.

Realms Underfoot is a role-playing game you play outdoors, through your phone. Creatures stand in the real park at their real size: a goblin comes up to your waist, and the dragon is eight metres long.

You get through each encounter your own way. Sneak past, talk, fight, or think it through.

Get on the list How it plays

For Android phones that support ARCore. Not out yet.

Four ways through

  • Sneak

    Crouch, keep quiet and stay out of sight. The game hears your microphone and knows when you duck.

  • Talk

    Say it out loud. Flatter them, lie to them, threaten them or ask about the box. They answer.

  • Fight

    Swing, block, and time a parry. Then watch for the rocks.

  • Think

    Some things cannot be beaten with a stick. One of them only moves when you are not looking at her.
